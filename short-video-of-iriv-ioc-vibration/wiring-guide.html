Commissioning checklist

  • Confirm sensor model, firmware build and all terminal labels.
  • Mount sensor securely; identify axes; support cable clear of rotating parts.
  • With power isolated, check continuity, polarity, commons, shield and termination.
  • Confirm supply voltage at the sensor and IOC under load.
  • Match baud, parity, stop bits and the configured sensor ID.
  • Check fresh readings and sensor identity in the gateway dashboard.
  • Configure broker, topic and credentials; verify readings at the receiver.
  • Choose machine-specific limits, delay and hysteresis.
  • Test alarm, acknowledgement while still high, return-to-normal and sensor-loss behavior.
  • Record baseline, operating conditions, configuration and test results.
